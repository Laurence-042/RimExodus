(The mod description has a character limit, so this is a detailed supplement.)

---

- 天气被强制修改后，若修改源未处理（如游戏条件未结束）其所在地图就被删除，天气将无法恢复——恢复途径随修改源一起被删了。
If the weather is force-modified and the source of the modification (e.g. an unfinished game condition) is on a map that gets deleted before it clears up, the weather can never return to normal — the restoration path was deleted along with the source.

严格来说，这个mod对这个问题做过一些防护，但是仍然不建议在天气控制器、仪式导致异常天气期间删除地图，以免产生问题。
Strictly speaking, this mod does have some safeguards against this, but it is still not recommended to delete maps during abnormal weather caused by weather controllers or rituals, to avoid problems.

---

- 挖矿/砍伐等"designation 前置型"命令不出现在跨图右键菜单（走近过缝后一切正常）。
"Designation-first" commands such as mining and chopping don't appear in cross-map right-click menus (everything works normally once you've walked across the seam).

这是因为相关的跨图菜单会要求patch远比现在多的地方，考虑到可维护性没有硬写。而且完全可以多走几步到对应地图上正常执行操作，不需要强求跨图操作。
This is because cross-map menus for these would require patching far more places than the current implementation does, and it was deliberately skipped for maintainability. Besides, you can simply walk a few steps onto that map and order normally there — cross-map operation isn't really necessary.

---

- 不会做跨图搬运等跨图工作支持。
Cross-map hauling and other cross-map work support will not be implemented.

这个mod设计背景是为了让Odyssey更Odyssey，而不是建造超大基地。
This mod is designed to make Odyssey more Odyssey, not to build one giant megabase.

---

- Odyssey 空间层地图与各类口袋图（Pocket Map，比如地下仓库、巨坑、 VMF 的载具内部图之类的）不参与表面无缝语义，保持原版行为。
Odyssey space-layer maps and pocket maps (underground vaults, pits, VMF vehicle interiors, etc.) stay outside the surface seamless system and keep vanilla behavior.

无缝在毫无内容的外太空移动毫无意义。而地下部分、载具地图之类的并没有绑定的星球坐标，无法合理做切分。而且口袋地图通常语义上也不适合做无缝。
Seamless movement through empty outer space is meaningless. Underground maps and vehicle maps have no bound planet coordinates, so there is no sensible way to carve them. And pocket maps generally don't fit the seamless concept semantically either.

- 派系基地交易仅支持随身物品（地上的物品、随行囚犯/奴隶出售暂不支持）。
Settlement trading only supports carried items (items on the ground and selling accompanying prisoners/slaves are not supported yet).

这个其实是一个妥协设计。
This is actually a compromise design.

这个派系基地交易的本质其实是维护了一个不可见、不可被埋伏、和当前地块上的殖民者成员对应的影子远行队。殖民者身上的东西会直接被算作影子远行队的库存，所以能直接售卖。但是存储区的物品和囚犯/奴隶就没有这个待遇了。
Under the hood, settlement trading maintains an invisible, ambush-immune shadow caravan matching the colonists on the current tile. Items on a colonist count directly as the shadow caravan's inventory, so they can be sold. Items in stockpiles and prisoners/slaves don't get this treatment.

不过我想这也不是一个大问题。
But I don't think this is a big deal.

如果你使用穿梭机，那么只需要别进入派系基地，作为真远行队交易就行了。
If you use shuttles, just don't enter the settlement — trade as a real caravan instead.

如果使用 Vehicle Framework 的载具，它们也会被视为远行队的一员，所以其后备箱里的东西也会被视为可以交易的随身物品。
With Vehicle Framework vehicles, the vehicle counts as a caravan member too, so items in its trunk count as tradable carried items.

而且应该没人想麻醉囚犯后扛着到处跑，或者带着一堆奴隶去探索世界吧？如果真的要卖奴隶……先用原版远行队吧。
And surely nobody wants to carry unconscious prisoners around while exploring, or travel the world with a crowd of slaves? If you really must sell slaves… use a vanilla caravan for that.

- 分帧生成邻接地图的过程中（进度显示在屏幕左上角）无法存档。
Saving is blocked while an adjacent map generates frame-by-frame (progress shows in the top-left corner).

我寻思着不是啥问题
I don't consider this much of a problem.

---

- 邻居地图渲染为近似实现（无阴影/水波等细节），跨天气域的邻居天色跟随当前图。
Neighbor maps are rendered approximately (no shadows/ripples and similar details), and neighbors in another weather domain follow the current map's sky.

都是为了性能考虑，RimWorld的天气特效说实话挺费fps的，而fps消耗过大也会影响tps
This is all for performance: RimWorld's weather effects are honestly quite FPS-hungry, and heavy FPS load drags TPS down too.

---

- 星球上的12个五边形地块周围一圈的六边形地图连接视觉上会有点问题，但不影响跨图移动。
Hexagon maps around the planet's 12 pentagon tiles have some visual issues at their connections, but cross-map movement is unaffected.

这是因为五边形附近的投影畸变更加严重，3格宽的接缝无法吸收这个畸变了（实际上即使是普通六边形地图也会因为投影畸变有些许旋转，但一般不会超过接缝范围）
This is because projection distortion is worse near pentagons, and the 3-cell-wide seam band can no longer absorb it (in fact even regular hexagon maps get slight rotation from projection distortion, but it usually stays within the seam band).

但因为投影总是以当前地块的表现为优先考虑的，所以聚焦对应地块后会发现原本断裂的地方是拼好的，不影响实际移动
Since projection always prioritizes the currently focused tile, once you focus the tile in question you'll find the previously broken spot is stitched together — actual movement is unaffected.

---

- 步行袭击现在会从你尚未看见的方向进场（无图边缘或邻图外侧），敌对袭击（含发狂动物、食尸鬼、奇美拉）可在邻图外侧进场后跨缝行军攻来。边界：从邻图行军过来的袭击者不会绑架/偷窃（与跨缝追击的敌人同语义）；袭击超时计时包含行军时间；行军途经图上有你的殖民者/建筑时会先与本地交战（涌现行为）；邪教徒围攻与访客/旅行者暂不参与外缘进场。空投/钻地等非步行进场不受影响。可在设置"跨图战斗"页关闭回到原版落点。
Walking raids now enter from directions you haven't seen (an edge with no map beyond, or a neighboring map's outer edge); hostile attacks (manhunter animals, ghouls, and chimeras included) can enter beyond an adjacent map and march across the seam to attack. Boundaries: raiders marching in from a neighboring map don't kidnap or steal (same rule as enemies chasing across seams); the raid timeout clock includes their march time; if their route passes a map with your colonists or buildings, they engage there first (emergent behavior); cult sieges and visitors/travelers don't use outer-edge entry yet. Drop pods, tunnels, and other non-walking arrivals are unaffected. Can be disabled in the "Cross-map combat" settings tab to restore vanilla spawn locations.

外缘进场的敌人走的是与跨缝追击/撤离同一条跨图移动链，而绑架、偷窃这类行为挂在原版的袭击结算上，跨图行军中不会执行；袭击计时在生成时就开始走，行军自然算在内。近战敌人（发狂动物、食尸鬼、蹒跚怪、奇美拉、近战袭击者）同样跨图索敌——锁定邻图目标后桥接行军过缝再打。mod 添加的袭击事件默认也跨图：无法识别的策略在迁移时降级为普通突击（专属策略行为会丢失），不希望某个事件跨图可在设置的"袭击事件类型"列表里把它改回本图生成；机械体围攻/灵能仪式围攻与邪教徒围攻不参与跨图迁移（自建营地机制限定在自己图上）。
Enemies entering from the outer edge use the same cross-map movement chain as pursuit and evacuation across seams, and behaviors like kidnapping or stealing are tied to vanilla raid resolution, so they don't execute during a cross-map march; the raid clock starts at spawn, so the march time counts. Melee enemies (manhunter animals, ghouls, shamblers, chimeras, melee raiders) acquire targets across maps too — they lock onto a target on the neighboring map, march across the seam, and fight. Mod-added raid incidents also default to cross-map: unrecognized strategies are downgraded to a plain assault when relocated (their unique strategy behavior is lost) — if you don't want that for a specific incident, switch it back to local spawning in the settings' incident-type list; mechanoid sieges, psychic ritual sieges, and cult sieges never relocate (their camp-building mechanics are bound to their own map).

---

- 爆炸范围伤害不跨缝：弹丸（含迫击炮/手雷）可以飞过接缝，但爆炸只在落点地图按原生范围结算。
Explosions don't cross seams: projectiles (mortar shells and grenades included) can fly across a seam, but the blast only resolves on the landing map at its native radius.

设计如此：让爆炸跨缝需要接管原版爆炸的逐格结算并按格归属图路由，patch 面和兼容性风险都不小。弹丸本身能跨缝、落点图是对的，绝大多数场景已经成立，我不想为边缘情形冒这个险。
By design: making blasts cross seams would mean taking over vanilla's per-cell explosion resolution and routing each cell to its owning map — a large patch surface and a real compatibility risk. The projectile itself crosses the seam and lands on the right map, which already covers the vast majority of cases; I'd rather not take that risk for the edge cases.

---

- 跨图寻路/下令是单跳的：一次下令最多到达相邻一张地图，多段行程需要逐图下令。
Cross-map pathfinding/orders are single-hop: one order reaches at most the adjacent map; longer trips must be ordered map by map.

设计如此：一张 RimWorld 地图的运行开销不算小，我不建议同时开很多地图，多跳下令等于鼓励这么玩。而且多跳意味着沿途地图要一并加载渲染，还得大改摄像机逻辑，容易和 Simple Camera Settings 之类的 mod 冲突——逐图下令麻烦一点，但可控。
By design: a single RimWorld map isn't cheap to run, and I don't recommend keeping many maps loaded — multi-hop orders would encourage exactly that. Multi-hop would also mean loading and rendering every map along the route plus substantial camera work, likely conflicting with mods like Simple Camera Settings. Ordering map by map takes a few more clicks, but stays in control.

---

- 武器射程曲线映射（默认关）的边界：Combat Extended 武器射程同样映射，但 CE 迫击炮 75 格直瞄/间瞄分界是 CE 硬编码、不随曲线移动；原版恶劣天气的射程截断仍按原版语义生效；武器最小射程不映射；以 rangeStat（按属性定义射程）定义射程的罕见 verb 与挂在小体积 Def（hediff/能力）上的罕见弹丸 verb 不在映射范围；射程超过 90 格时原版不再绘制选中圈（纯视觉）。
Weapon range curve remap (off by default) boundaries: Combat Extended weapon ranges are remapped too, but CE's hardcoded 75-cell direct/indirect mortar threshold doesn't move with the curve; vanilla bad-weather range truncation still applies as usual; minimum ranges are not remapped; rare verbs with rangeStat-defined ranges and rare projectile verbs attached to small defs (hediffs/abilities) are outside the remap; ranges beyond 90 cells lose their selection circle (vanilla behavior, visual only).

这些边界要么是外部系统写死的逻辑，要么是极罕见的定义方式：CE 的迫击炮 75 格分界是 CE 自己硬编码的，原版恶劣天气的射程截断是原版语义，硬改就得去 patch 两边的内部实现；以 rangeStat 或挂在小体积 Def 上的弹丸 verb 定义的射程太少见，为它们扩大映射覆盖面不划算。
These boundaries are either hard-coded by external systems or extremely rare definition styles: CE's 75-cell mortar threshold is hard-coded in CE, and vanilla bad-weather range truncation is vanilla semantics — forcing either means patching their internals. Ranges defined via rangeStat or projectile verbs on tiny defs are too rare to be worth widening the remap's surface for.

---

- Combat Extended 的世界炮击不进入跨图弹道层，继续使用 CE 原生世界对象流程。
Combat Extended world shelling stays outside the cross-map projectile layer and keeps using CE's native world-object flow.

CE 的世界炮击走的是它自己的世界对象流程（发射 → 世界地图 → 目标图），本来就不经过地图接缝；把它塞进跨图弹道层意味着要复刻 CE 的世界流程逻辑，风险大而收益小。
CE's world shelling goes through its own world-object flow (fire → world map → target map) and never passes through a map seam in the first place; pulling it into the cross-map projectile layer would mean reimplementing CE's world-side logic — high risk, little gain.

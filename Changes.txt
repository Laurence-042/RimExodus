0.1.5
- 修复激活mod时太空地图背景被清除为黑色的bug
Fixed a bug where the space map background was cleared to black while the mod was active
- 邻接生成的地图保证从地图中心到接缝有一条不被岩石阻挡的走廊（只影响岩石生成，不改动地形）；走廊端点按邻图接缝带的可达性分集合选取，多个已生成邻居的地图会生成连通各方向的洞穴网络
Maps generated as neighbors are now guaranteed a rock-free corridor from the map center to the seam (only affects rock generation, never modifies terrain); corridor endpoints are selected by grouping cells on each neighboring map's seam band by reachability, and maps with multiple generated neighbors get a cave network connecting all directions
- 重构河流的接缝穿越机制：河道中线 = 两个确定性穿越点（对称哈希或邻接地图实际记录，两侧地图恒得同一点）的连线并延伸到地图边界，截断楔口不再出现在接缝带；河流生成时不进入 void 区域（外条带快照不再携带河水），河流实际修改的格子受接缝混合保护；道路穿越点同样改为确定性偏移（不再固定边中点）且末端强制垂直于接缝
Reworked the river seam crossing mechanism: the river centerline is the line through two deterministic crossing points (symmetric hash or the neighboring map's recorded position — both maps always agree) extended to the map border, eliminating truncation wedges near seams; rivers no longer generate into void regions (outer-strip snapshots no longer carry river water), and cells actually modified by rivers are protected from seam blending; road crossings now also use deterministic offsets instead of the fixed edge midpoint, with ends forced perpendicular to the seam
- 已知限制：Geological Landforms 的河流地貌会整体替换原版河流生成且无法对齐跨图接缝，需在其设置中禁用全部河流地貌（详见 README 兼容性表）
Known limitation: Geological Landforms river landforms replace vanilla river generation entirely and cannot be aligned across map seams; disable all river landforms in its settings (see the compatibility table in the README)
